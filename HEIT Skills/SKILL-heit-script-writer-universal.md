# HEIT Script Writer

**Source:** Dan Martell Elite Coaching (official framework)
**Format:** Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts (60 seconds or under)

Viral content isn't luck. It's structure.

The HEIT framework turns any idea, insight, or raw talking point into a short-form
video script that stops the scroll, makes your audience feel seen, builds authority,
and drives revenue.

---

## Step 0 — Load Your Voice (before anything else)

Structure without a voice is dead on camera. Before writing, load the creator's own
voice or style guide (paste it into the session, or point to the doc). Pull out:
- their signature hooks and closing lines, verbatim
- the analogies they naturally reach for
- words they use and words they ban
- stories they have actually lived

If no guide exists, ask for three things: three posts that performed, one story they
tell often, and five words they'd never use. If the guide contradicts current facts
(an old title, an outdated product list, old brand colours), current facts win.
Note the correction for the creator.

---

## The Framework

### H — HOOK (first 3 seconds)
If your content could apply to anyone, it connects with no one.
Specificity is what creates connection. Specificity drives distribution.

Three hook types — pick one per script:

**Context Hook** — State exactly what you're teaching.
> "Here's the one thing most $500K service businesses get wrong about their first hire."

**Contrarian Hook** — Say something people will push back on.
> "Your bottleneck isn't your team. It's your process."

**Intrigue Hook** — Open a loop they have to close.
> "This founder added $400K in revenue without hiring a single person."


---

### E — EMOTION
People don't share information. They share how something made them feel.

Your job isn't to be clever. It's to be accurate.
Say what your audience is already experiencing — better than they can articulate it.

The filter: "Would someone feel understood reading this?"
If not — rewrite it.

Emotion to aim for: friction, relief, validation, or urgency.
Without one of those, it won't spread.

> "You hired your first operator thinking it would buy back your time.
> Now you have a new bottleneck — with a salary attached."

**The creator's beat (required, inside Emotion or Insight):** one first-person story
they lived, an opinion they'd defend in a room, or an analogy that makes the abstract
physical. If a script has no story and no analogy, it isn't finished.

---

### I — INSIGHT
Emotion gets attention. Insight builds trust.
Trust is what turns content into clients.

Go beneath the surface. Not what everyone already knows.
The nuanced truth your audience hasn't heard articulated this clearly before.

Generic content gets ignored. Nuanced content gets shared.

> "The bottleneck didn't move. It just got more expensive.
> Because the hire inherited your process — and your process was the problem."

---

### T — TAKEAWAY
Every post should leave someone with a shift in thinking or a clear next step.

One thing. Not five. One.
That's what creates: conversations, opportunities, sales.

> "Before your next hire, document the process they're walking into.
> If you can't document it, you're not ready to delegate it."

**Reflective close:** the last spoken line before the CTA can be a question that
hands the problem back to the viewer, in the creator's own style.

Always close with your CTA on its own line. Only use "Comment [WORD]" CTAs if
comment-to-DM automation is actually set up; otherwise use "link in bio."

## Before You Write — Non-Negotiable Steps

These are Dan Martell's homework steps from the April 15, 2026 Elite session.
Do not skip them. They are the inputs that make the framework work.

### Step 1 — Define Your Target
Get clear on two things:
- Your ICP's revenue level (e.g. $250K, $500K, $1M+)
- Your ICP's team size (e.g. solo, 1-5 employees, 5-15 employees)

Name the specific person. Not a demographic. A human with a specific problem.
Example: *Sarah runs a $700K marketing agency with 4 employees. She's the only
one who can close new clients and she knows that's killing her growth.*

### Step 2 — Generate 25 Challenges
Run this prompt (in any LLM):

"Make a list of 25 nuanced, observable challenges that a business owner
that has [REVENUE] revenue and [TEAM SIZE] employees struggles with."

Each item on that list is a potential script.
Pick 1-5 to build into HEIT posts. Start there.

## Writing the Script

### Step 1: Name the ICP at the top
One line. Specific person, revenue, team size, core problem.

### Step 2: Pick the challenge from your list
State which of the 25 challenges this script addresses.

### Step 3: Choose the hook type
- Stat or specific situation — Contrarian or Intrigue
- Process or framework — Context
- Opinion or positioning — Contrarian

### Step 4: Write one thought per line
No paragraphs. Teleprompter-ready.
~2 seconds per line at natural speaking pace.
Target: 25-35 lines for 60 seconds.

### Step 4b: Write a beat sheet
Most people record better talking than reading. Under the script, add 5 bullets to
talk through: hook (verbatim), receipt, story/analogy, the one insight, the closing
question (verbatim).

If the creator gave a voice memo, transcript or rough notes, keep their phrasing.
Restructure into HEIT; don't rewrite it into generic copy.

### Step 5: Check HEIT before you share
- H: Does the first line stop the scroll?
- E: Does it make your audience feel seen?
- I: Does it teach something nuanced they haven't heard this way?
- T: Is the takeaway one clear shift or action?
- Voice: Is there one first-person story, opinion or analogy? Would the creator say every line out loud?
- CTA: Is it on its own line, clean, no friction?

## Writing Rules

- Short declaratives. One idea per line. No padding.
- Lead with what others aren't saying.
- Say what your audience is already experiencing — better than they can say it.
- If it's safe, it won't spread. Be honest. Call things out.
- One CTA per script. On its own line. No enthusiasm required.
- Generic gets ignored. Nuanced gets shared.
- The script is about the viewer's problem, told through the creator's experience.
