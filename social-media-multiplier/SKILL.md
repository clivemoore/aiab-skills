# Social Media Content Multiplier

Given a core content idea, draft, or topic, produce ready-to-post versions for ALL 7 platforms. Never skip a platform.

## Voice & Style

Before writing content, load the full voice reference:

```
Read references/voice-guide.md
```

**Quick voice essentials** (the reference file has complete details):
- Contrarian, direct, short punchy paragraphs
- No buzzwords: never "synergy," "leverage" (as verb), "disrupt," "revolutionary," "game-changing"
- Vocabulary: infrastructure, orchestration, amplify, human-in-the-loop, workflow, workalongs (never "agents" for Workilo products)
- Opening hooks: "Nobody wants to talk about...", "Everyone's asking [X]. That misses the point."
- Closing: end with a question that hands the problem back to the reader
- No emojis unless specifically requested
- Anti-hustle culture — substance over polish, infrastructure over magic

## Content Mix Guidance

When planning content, aim for this weekly ratio:
- 40% Niche Specific — your lane, your expertise, speaks to your one person
- 25% Viral/Growth — broad, relatable, shareable, opens new rooms
- 20% Authority — client wins, results, frameworks, receipts
- 15% Personality — behind the scenes, dogs, the human part

Posts that hit all four at once are "unicorns." Aim for those.

## Platform Specs

### 1. LINKEDIN
- Professional with contrarian edge. Full thought leadership.
- 800-1300 chars. Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences). White space between.
- Hashtags: 4-6 in FIRST COMMENT only, never in post body. From: #AIEthics #AIAgents #HumanInTheLoop #EnterpriseAI #ProductLeadership #BuildDifferent
- Images: YES — Portrait (1080×1350) or square (1080×1080) preferred.
- Tags: Workilo, GPT Studio, Agency in a Box in body or first comment.
- Links: "Link in comments" — never URLs in post body.
- Close with a question or reflection.

### 2. FACEBOOK
- Casual-professional. Community-oriented. Shorter than LinkedIn.
- Hashtags: Optional, 2-3 max at end. Links: "Link in comments" preferred.

### 3. INSTAGRAM
- Visual-first. Caption supports image/video.
- Hashtags: 6-10 in FIRST COMMENT only. Images: REQUIRED (1080×1350 or 1080×1080).
- Video: Post as Reel (30-90 sec ideal). Tag @jenwhitneyburton for Dan Martell content.
- Links: "Link in bio" only.

### 4. THREADS
- Lowercase conversational. Short to medium. NEVER hashtags.

### 5. X / TWITTER
- Punchy, compressed. 280 char HARD LIMIT. 1-2 hashtags max, optional.
- Tags: @Workilo_io, @AgencyinaboxOS. Images: Landscape (1200×675).

### 6. BLUESKY
- Lowercase conversational. 300 char limit per post. THREAD if needed.
- NEVER hashtags. Tag @workilo.bsky.social when relevant.

### 7. EH.SOCIAL (ehnow.ca)
- Lowercase conversational. Canadian Mastodon instance. NEVER hashtags.
- Canadian context resonates well.

## Trial Reel Strategy (Instagram)

Every Instagram output includes two text-overlay hooks for A/B testing via Trial Reels:

1. Write two hooks for the same content — different angle, different hook type
2. User records one reel, duplicates it in editor, swaps the text overlay
3. Posts both as Trial Reels (toggle "Trial" ON — shown only to non-followers)
4. After 24-48h, check "Follows from this reel" in insights
5. Winner gets promoted to main feed via "Share to everyone"

Requirements: public professional account, 1K+ followers, 5 trials/day max.
Without trial access: post both variants to main feed normally.

**Repost rule:** Never post a great video only once. After 30 days, repost with
a new hook. One great video = 10+ pieces of content.
