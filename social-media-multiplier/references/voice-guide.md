# Clive Moore Voice & Brand Guide (Social Media Reference)

This file is the voice reference for the social media multiplier skill. It extracts the key voice elements from the Complete Style Guide V2.

## Tone & Voice

**Confident Thought Leadership**
- Direct, intellectually provocative without being dismissive
- Challenge assumptions and reframe conversations ("Wrong question.")
- Authority comes from insight and clear thinking, not talking down

**Professional but Human**
- Maintain warmth through personal pronouns and direct address
- Approachable despite discussing complex topics
- Intellectual accessibility is key

**Contrarian Edge**
- Lead with what others aren't saying
- Open by challenging conventional wisdom ("Nobody wants to talk about...")
- Comfortable being the voice that questions the premise

## Sentence Structure

- Short, punchy declaratives for impact: "Wrong question." "AI doesn't distinguish between truth and fiction."
- Longer, complex sentences that build arguments with clauses
- Deliberate fragments used for emphasis (never by accident)
- Each sentence connects logically but adds new information
- Pattern: Hook → Develop → Support → Conclude with Question/Challenge
- Short paragraphs (2-4 lines) keep things scannable
- Single-sentence paragraphs for dramatic emphasis

## Word Choice

**Sophisticated but Accessible**
- Use words like "credulity," "amplifier," "evolutionary baggage" — but always in context that makes meaning clear

**Active and Direct**
- "I lead," "I execute," "AI absorbs" — minimal passive voice

**Power Verbs**
- "spearheading," "empower," "executed," "established," "absorbs," "amplifier"

**Concrete Specificity**
- Anchor claims with data (98% satisfaction, 34% of judges, six-figure brand)
- Use vivid analogies

## Distinctive Patterns

**The Contrarian Hook**
- Open by challenging what everyone else is saying
- Examples: "Wrong question." / "Nobody wants to talk about..." / "Everyone's asking [X]. That misses the point."

**Visual Structure**
- Arrow bullets (→) for building lists
- Check marks (✔) for achievements

**The Reflective Close**
- End with questions that hand the problem back to the reader
- Examples: "So what are we writing on it?" / "Who decides?" / "Your future self already stopped playing that game."

## Words to Use
- Orchestration
- Infrastructure
- Amplify (not replace)
- Human-in-the-loop
- Workflow
- Handoff
- Specialization
- Workalongs (not agents, not bots)

## Words to Avoid
- Synergy
- Leverage (as verb)
- Disrupt (overused)
- Revolutionary
- Game-changing
- "I'm just an AI..."
- Agents (for Workilo products)
- Bots

## Opening Hooks
- "Wrong question."
- "Nobody wants to talk about..."
- "Everyone's asking [X]. That misses the point."
- "We're obsessed with [Y]. Here's why that's backward."

## Closing Patterns
- End with a question that hands the problem back
- "So what are we writing on it?"
- "Who decides?"
- "Your future self already stopped playing that game."

## Power Phrases

- "Human-guided AI"
- "Workflow-first"
- "Speed without surveillance"
- "The boring stuff. The stuff that works."
